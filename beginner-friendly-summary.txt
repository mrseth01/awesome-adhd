Awesome ADHD - beginner-friendly resources and starter path
Source: README.md

This short list pulls out five easy starting points from the full README.
The order below goes from quick self-check, to understanding, to practical next steps.

1. ADHD Test (Psych Central)
Link: https://psychcentral.com/quizzes/adhd-quiz/
Why it is beginner-friendly: It is fast and gives you a simple first checkpoint before you dive deeper.

2. ADHD in Adults: Understanding Why You Do What You Do (ADDitude)
Link: https://www.additudemag.com/adhd-in-adults-nervous-system/?mc_cid=d36f2091a2&mc_eid=22cc69c9da
Why it is beginner-friendly: It explains common ADHD patterns in plain language and helps make the experience feel less confusing.

3. This is how you treat ADHD based off science
Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_tpB-B8BXk0&list=PLWDDHvZekEYyMev-VD53kakxbHpFbtshX&index=6&t=0s
Why it is beginner-friendly: It gives a practical overview of treatment ideas without requiring a lot of background knowledge.

4. Failing at Normal: An ADHD Success Story | Jessica McCabe | TEDxBratislava
Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JiwZQNYlGQI
Why it is beginner-friendly: It is approachable, encouraging, and easy to connect to if you are just starting to learn about ADHD.

5. Tips for Managing Adult ADHD/ADD: Deal with ADHD Symptoms and Become More Focused and Organized (HelpGuide)
Link: https://www.helpguide.org/articles/add-adhd/managing-adult-adhd-attention-deficit-disorder.htm
Why it is beginner-friendly: It turns the big topic into concrete day-to-day habits for focus, organization, and routine.

Suggested order:
1. Take the quiz.
2. Read the ADDitude article.
3. Watch one of the two videos.
4. End with the HelpGuide article for practical next actions.

30-minute starter path

Goal:
Get a fast, low-stress introduction to ADHD using the 5 resources above.

Ground rule:
Set a 30-minute timer and move on when the time block ends. The goal is not to finish everything. The goal is to get oriented.

Minutes 0-3
Resource: ADHD Test (Psych Central)
What to do: Take the quiz quickly. Do not overthink the answers.
What to notice: Does the result make you want to learn more or seek a proper evaluation?

Minutes 3-9
Resource: ADHD in Adults: Understanding Why You Do What You Do (ADDitude)
What to do: Read the intro and skim for parts that sound familiar.
What to notice: Write down 1 or 2 lines that feel like they describe your experience.

Minutes 9-15
Resource: This is how you treat ADHD based off science
What to do: Watch until this time block ends.
What to notice: Pick 1 treatment idea or explanation that feels useful or reassuring.

Minutes 15-21
Resource: Failing at Normal: An ADHD Success Story | Jessica McCabe | TEDxBratislava
What to do: Watch part of the talk for a more human and relatable view.
What to notice: Write down 1 sentence that makes you feel understood or less alone.

Minutes 21-30
Resource: Tips for Managing Adult ADHD/ADD: Deal with ADHD Symptoms and Become More Focused and Organized (HelpGuide)
What to do: Skim the article and choose only 1 practical step to try this week.
Good first choices:
- Put one recurring task on a calendar reminder.
- Break one task into smaller steps.
- Choose one time of day to reset your space for 5 minutes.

Finish line:
At the end of 30 minutes, keep only these 3 things:
1. One idea that sounded like you.
2. One idea that helped explain ADHD.
3. One small action to try this week.

If you want a second session later:
- Finish either of the two videos.
- Re-read the HelpGuide article and pick a second action.
